# :video_game: ***The Final Feather***

Themes:
- Time
- Light and Darkness
- Growth
- Last of its kind

**Genre:** Horde Survival
**Platform:** PC/Web
**Target Audience:** People who enjoy to break records in games

:dart: **Core Concept**

The player controls a duck, the last one of its kind, agains endless hordes of creatures. As time passes, stronger and more numerous enemies appear. The player must survive for as long as possible, unlocking new abilities, and aim to beat their personal record

:joystick: ***Core Mechanics***

- **Player Movement: **Free movement in the limited map
- **Energy & Abilities: **The player has an energy bar; abilities consume energy and recharge over time
- **Hordes & Scaling: **Enemies spawn in waves, increasing in strength and quantity as time goes on
- **Scoring System: **Players earn points for each enemy defeated
- **Progression: **Abilities unlock as hordes are defeated; The light will start to decrease
- **End Condition: **Game ends when the player dies

1. **Survive incoming horde**
2. **Defeat enemies -> earn points **
3. **Unlock new ability after surviving a set of hordes.**
